# The YBDC — complete user manual

**Feature status:** see [FEATURE_STATUS.md](./FEATURE_STATUS.md) for what is built, what is missing, and what we changed from your property PDF specifications.

This is the full guide for people who use the website. It walks you from your first visit through finding a property, listing one, booking a service, working as an agent, and working as a service partner.

It does **not** cover staff admin screens. If you are a customer, owner, tenant, agent, or service partner, this is the document for you.

**The YBDC** stands for Yashas Business Development & Consultancy.

## What you can do on the website

- **Search** approved listings: homes for rent or resale, PG and hostel rooms, flatmate rooms, commercial spaces, and land or plots.
- **Enquire** about a listing. The owner or agent receives your message.
- **List** your own property. It stays private until it is reviewed and approved.
- **Book** services: home repairs, packing and moving, painting and cleaning, IT, events, general help, job consultancy, and loans.
- **Work as a partner.** After verification you receive jobs, update the customer, and see earnings in your wallet.

## Chapter 2. Create your account

You need an account to send a property enquiry, post a listing, or track a service request. You can still read public pages without an account.

### 2.1 Register as a customer or property owner

1. Click **Register**.
2. Enter your **full name**.
3. Enter your **mobile number**. A 10-digit Indian number is accepted. The site adds the country code for you if you do not type it.
4. Click **Send OTP**.
5. Enter the code from the SMS.
6. Confirm. You are signed in.

If you already have an account, use **Sign in** with the same phone number and a new OTP. You do not need a password for phone login.

### 2.2 Finish your profile (first login)

The first time you sign in, you may see **Complete Your Profile**. Every field on this screen is required.

- **Email**
- **Name**
- **Address**
- **City**
- **State**
- **Country**

You can click **Use current location** to fill the address from your device, then edit anything that looks wrong. If location permission is blocked, type the address yourself.

Click save. You are taken to your dashboard.

### 2.3 Register as a service partner

Use the partner registration page (linked from the home page, contact page, or **Register as a partner**).

1. Enter your **name**.
2. Enter your **business name**.
3. Choose one or more **service categories**.
4. Enter your **mobile number** and complete **OTP**.

You then complete onboarding (email and address) the same way as a customer. After that, open **Profile** and finish business details and documents. The full partner path is in [Chapter 7](#chapter-7-for-service-partners).

### 2.4 Property agents

Agents sign in with the same phone OTP. If your organisation asked you to verify as an agent, follow the **Verify agent** link you were given before you start posting listings.

### 2.5 Sign out

Open your name menu and choose **Sign out**.

## Chapter 3. Find a property

This chapter is the full path from search to enquiry.

### 3.1 Where listings come from

Only **approved** listings appear in search. A listing an owner just submitted is **Pending** and is hidden from everyone else until it is approved.

### 3.2 Search from the home page

1. On the home page, use the search area or open a featured listing.
2. Click a property card to open the full page.
3. The card usually shows a photo, title, price, location, and a short label such as rent, sale, or the property category.

### 3.3 Browse after you sign in

1. Sign in.
2. Open **Properties → Browse**.
3. You can switch between a **list** and a **map**.
4. Use filters to narrow results. Typical filters include:
   - City or locality
   - Rent or sale
   - Sector (residential, commercial, land/plot) when available
   - Listing type (rent, resale, PG, flatmates, sale)
   - Budget
   - BHK and furnishing where those apply to the listing

Apply the filters, then open a card.

### 3.4 Read a listing

On the property page you should see:

- Photos (and video or floor plans when the owner added them)
- Price (rent per month, sale price, or lease amount)
- Location and map
- Description
- Key facts for that listing type (BHK, area, furnishing, plot size, commercial type, and so on)
- Amenities
- A contact area for the listing representative

You can copy or share the page link with someone else. They see the public page. They still need their own account to send an enquiry.

### 3.5 Send an enquiry — start to finish

1. Open the listing.
2. If you are not signed in, the site asks you to **sign in** or **register**. After login you return to the listing.
3. Open **Send enquiry** (or the contact card).
4. Type a short message. Example: “I can visit this Saturday morning. Is the flat still available?”
5. Send the enquiry.
6. The owner or agent receives it under their **Leads**. They contact you using the details on your account.

You do not need to call a number shown on the listing if the enquiry form is available. Keep your phone and email on **Profile** up to date so they can reach you.

### 3.6 If you are the owner

The public page **List your property** explains the idea in four steps: add details, upload photos, set a price, receive enquiries. That page does not publish the listing by itself.

To actually publish:

1. **Register** or **Sign in**.
2. Open **My Listings**.
3. Click **Add Property**.
4. Follow [Chapter 4](#chapter-4-list-a-property).

## Chapter 4. List a property

Tenants (owners) and agents use the same form. Sign in first.

### 4.1 Open the form

1. In the sidebar, open **Properties → My Listings**.
2. If you have no listings, the page asks you to add one.
3. Click **Add Property**.

### 4.2 Choose the kind of listing

The first step has only two choices. **Next** stays off until both are selected.

**Sector**

- Residential
- Commercial
- Land/Plot

**Listing type** (depends on the sector)

| Sector | You can choose |
|--------|----------------|
| Residential | Rent, Resale, PG/Hostel, Flatmates |
| Commercial | Rent, Sale |
| Land/Plot | Resale |

Click **Next**. The rest of the form matches that pair. **Previous** goes back and keeps what you already typed.

### 4.3 Move through the steps

The left side lists the steps for your listing type. Typical order:

1. Listing type (create only)
2. Property details
3. Locality details
4. Rent, resale, or sale details
5. Amenities
6. Gallery
7. Extra information (documents, description, or contact preference — depends on the type)

On the **last** step, click **Create Property**.

While it saves, the button shows **Saving…**. When it succeeds you see **Property submitted** and you return to **My Listings**.

**Cancel** leaves the form and does not save.

If a required field is empty, the form returns to that step and names the field. Fill it and continue.

### 4.4 What happens after you submit

| What you see on My Listings | What visitors see | What you should do |
|-----------------------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| **Pending** | Nothing. The listing is hidden. | Wait for review. You may get an email when it is decided. |
| **Approved** | The listing appears in browse and on its public page. | Share the link. Watch for enquiries. |
| **Rejected** | Still hidden. | Read the reason, edit the listing, and save again. |

Staff listings can go live immediately. Your own listing as a tenant or agent always waits for review.

### 4.5 Edit a listing

1. Open **My Listings**.
2. Open the listing.
3. On edit, every section is on one page (not a step-by-step wizard).
4. Change the fields you need.
5. **Update Property** appears at the bottom only after you change something.
6. Save. If the save fails, your typing stays on the page.

### 4.6 Delete a listing

On **My Listings**, choose delete and confirm. The listing is removed. This cannot be undone from the same screen, so confirm you picked the right one.

### 4.7 Rules that apply to every new listing

- Give the listing a clear **title**. Example: “2 BHK in Saidapet, near the metro”.
- Set **city**, **locality**, and **street / landmark**.
- Drop the pin on the map in **Locality details**. Search for the society or a nearby landmark, or use your current location, then adjust the pin.
- Enter area in **square feet**.
- Set a price: monthly rent, sale price, lease amount, or the rent of your cheapest PG room.
- Photos and video are optional. Listings with photos receive more responses.
- In the **description**, write what is special about the property. **Do not** type your phone number or email in the description. People contact you through the enquiry on the site.

### 4.8 Older listings

Some listings created before these categories existed open a shorter form when you edit them: property type, rent / sale / lease, rooms, bathrooms, area, year built, and furnishing. New listings always use the sector and type steps in this chapter.

### 4.9 Residential — Rent (full walkthrough)

Use this when the home is for a tenant, either on monthly rent or on lease.

**Step A — Property details**

1. Title.
2. Apartment type:
   - **Apartment** — you also enter the apartment or society name, BHK (1, 2, 3, 4, or 4+), the floor the home is on, and the total floors in the building (Ground through 99).
   - **Gated Community Villa** — apartment or community name, BHK, and number of floors. Floor and total floors of a tower are hidden.
   - **Independent House/Villa** — BHK and **number of floors** (Ground only, or 1 to 5). Apartment name, floor, and total floors are hidden.
3. Property age: less than a year, 1–3 years, 3–5 years, 5–10 years, or more than 10 years.
4. Facing: North, South, East, West, the four corners, or Don’t know.
5. Built-up area in square feet.

**Step B — Locality details**

City, locality, landmark or street, and the map pin.

**Step C — Rent details**

1. **Property available for**
   - **Only rent** — monthly rent and deposit.
   - **Only lease** — rent and deposit are hidden. You enter the **lease amount** instead.
2. Tick **Rent negotiable** if you will discuss the rent. This is hidden for lease.
3. **Monthly maintenance**
   - Maintenance included, or
   - Maintenance extra — then enter the monthly maintenance amount.
4. **Available from** (date).
5. **Preferred tenants:** Anyone, Family, Bachelor Female, Bachelor Male, or Company.
6. **Furnishing:** Unfurnished, Semi-furnished, or Fully furnished. If it is semi or fully furnished, tick items that are included (for example TV and sofa).
7. **Parking:** Bike, Car, Both, or None.
8. Description.

**Step D — Amenities**

- Number of bathrooms (use plus and minus).
- Number of balconies.
- Water supply.
- Pet allowed (yes or no).
- Gym (yes or no).
- Non-veg allowed (yes or no).
- Gated security (yes or no).
- Who will show the property: Need Help, I will show, Neighbours, Friends/Relatives, Security, Tenants, or Others.
- Current condition: Vacant, Tenant on notice period, New property, or Need help to manage.
- Secondary phone number (optional).
- Whether you have more similar units.
- A short directions tip using landmarks.
- Tick building amenities such as lift, air conditioner, intercom, children play area, servant room, gas pipeline, rain water harvesting, housekeeping, visitor parking, internet, club house, swimming pool, fire safety, shopping centre, park, sewage treatment, and power backup.

**Step E — Gallery**

Add photos and, if you have them, a video and **floor plans**. Floor plans are asked on residential rent only.

Then click **Create Property**.

### 4.10 Residential — Resale (full walkthrough)

Use this when you are selling a home.

**Property details**

- Title.
- Apartment type: Apartment, Independent House/Villa, Gated Community Villa, or **Standalone Building**.
- The same show/hide rules as rent: houses hide floor and total floors and ask for number of floors; apartments and gated villas ask for the name.
- **Ownership:** Self owned, or On lease. If on lease, enter **lease years**.
- Built-up area (required) and carpet area (optional), both in square feet.
- Property age, including **Under construction**.
- Facing (required).
- Floor type: vitrified tiles, mosaic, marble/granite, wooden, or cement.

**Locality details** — same map and address as rent.

**Resale details**

- Expected price in rupees (required).
- Maintenance cost per month (required).
- Price negotiable.
- Currently under loan.
- Available from (required).
- Kitchen type: modular, covered shelves, or open shelves.
- Furnishing and parking (both required).
- Description. Do not put contact details in this box.

**Amenities**

- Bathrooms (required) and balconies.
- Gym and gated security (required).
- Who will show the property (required).
- Secondary number, similar units, directions for buyers.
- Tick amenities such as club house, lift, intercom, shopping centre, sewage treatment, gas pipeline, swimming pool, fire safety, children play area, park, visitor parking, and internet.

**Additional information**

Answer each document question with Yes, No, or Don’t know:

- Allotment letter
- Sale deed certificate
- Property tax paid
- Occupancy certificate

If sale deed is **No**, a further question appears: do you have a **sale agreement**?

**Gallery** — photos and video.

### 4.11 Residential — PG or hostel (full walkthrough)

**Room details**

1. Title.
2. Tick every room type you offer: **Single, Double, Three, Four**.
3. For **each** type you ticked, fill its own panel:
   - Expected rent per person
   - Expected deposit per person
   - Room amenities: cupboard, TV, bedding, geyser, AC, attached bathroom

**Locality details** — city, area, map.

**PG details**

- Place is available for: Male, Female, or Anyone.
- Preferred guests: Working professional, Student, or Both.
- Available from.
- Food included: Yes or No.
  - If Yes, tick Breakfast, Lunch, and/or Dinner.
  - Add food notes (for example weekdays only).

**PG rules**

The rule list changes with who can stay:

- Male: no smoking, no guardians stay, no girl’s entry, no drinking, no non-veg.
- Female: no smoking, no guardians stay, no boy’s entry, no drinking, no non-veg.
- Anyone: no smoking, no guardians stay, no drinking, no non-veg.

Also set **gate closing time** and a description. Do not put your phone number in the description.

**Amenities**

- Laundry: yes or no. If yes, add the timing.
- Room cleaning: yes or no. If yes, add the timing.
- Warden facility.
- Directions for tenants.
- Shared amenities: common TV, mess, lift, refrigerator, WiFi, cooking allowed, power backup.
- Parking: bike, car, both, or none.

**Gallery** — photos and video.

The price shown on the listing uses the **lowest** room rent you entered.

### 4.13 Land or plot — Resale (full walkthrough)

**Property details**

- Title.
- Plot area in square feet (required).
- Length and width in feet (optional). You can type the area yourself even if you also enter length and width.
- Boundary wall: yes or no (required).
- Floors allowed for construction.
- Corner plot: yes or no.
- Inside a gated project: yes or no.
- Facing.

**Locality details** — city, locality, street, map pin.

**Resale details**

- Expected price (required).
- Price negotiable.
- Available from (required).
- Currently under loan.
- Description.

**Amenities**

- Water supply: corporation, borewell, both, or none.
- Electricity: electricity board, solar, both, or none.
- Width of the facing road in feet.
- Gated security.

**Additional information**

- Ownership: freehold, leasehold, co-operative society, or power of attorney.
- Conversion certificate: yes, no, not needed, or don’t know.

**Gallery** — photos and video. Plot listings do not ask for a visit schedule.

### 4.14 Commercial — Rent (full walkthrough)

**Property details**

- Title.
- Property type, for example:
  - Office space
  - Co-working
  - Shop
  - Showroom
  - Godown / warehouse
  - Industrial shed
  - Industrial building
  - Restaurant / cafe
  - Other business
- Building type.
- Age of the property.
- Floor and total floors. The floor cannot be higher than the total.
- Super built-up area in square feet (required, greater than zero).
- Furnishing: unfurnished, semi-furnished, or fully furnished.
- On main road (yes or no).
- Corner property (yes or no).
- Whether you want WhatsApp updates.

**Locality details** — address and map pin. This is required so visitors can find the premises.

**Rent details**

- Expected rent (required).
- Rent negotiable.
- Security deposit (required).
- Deposit negotiable.
- Lease duration in months.
- Lock-in period in months.
- Available from (required).
- Maintenance amount and how often it is charged (monthly, quarterly, yearly, or included).
- Current status: vacant, occupied, or available soon.

**Amenities**

- Power backup: full, partial, need to arrange, or none.
- Lift: common, private, or none.
- Washroom: private, shared, or none.
- Water storage.
- Security.
- Wi-Fi.
- Parking. If parking is yes, enter the number of slots.

**Gallery** — photos and video.

**Additional information**

- Property description (required). Write at least a few clear sentences. Do not include your phone number.
- Other features (optional).
- Contact preference: phone, WhatsApp, or both (required).

### 4.16 Checklist before you click Create Property

- Sector and listing type are correct. Changing them later means different questions.
- Title, city, locality, and map pin are filled.
- Price is the real asking price, not a placeholder.
- Required yes/no and document questions are answered.
- At least a few photos are uploaded if you have them.
- Description has no phone number or email.
- You are ready for the listing to sit in **Pending** until it is approved.

## Chapter 5. Request a service

You can book a service even if you never list a property.

### 5.1 Pick the service

From the home page or the top menu, open the page you need:

| You need | Open |
|----------|------|
| Shifting home or office | Packers & Movers |
| Paint or a deep clean | Painting & Cleaning |
| Plumber, electrician, carpenter, repairs | Home Services |
| Everyday help and assembly | General Services |
| Laptop, network, CCTV | IT Services |
| A function or office event | Event Management |
| A job or hiring help | Job Consultancy |
| A loan | Loan Assistance, then the loan type |

Loan types on the site include **personal**, **home**, **vehicle**, and **mortgages**. Open the one that matches your need. Each page has its own form.

### 5.2 Fill the form

Typical questions (the exact fields change by service):

- What you need, in a short description
- Where the work should happen (area or address)
- A preferred date
- Your phone number (taken from your account when you are signed in)

Read the page, complete every required field, and submit.

If you are not signed in, the site asks you to sign in or register before the request is saved, so you can see it later.

### 5.3 What happens next

1. Your request is saved with a starting status (for example submitted or new).
2. The platform reviews it and may assign a **verified partner**.
3. The partner sees the job in their **Leads**. They may not see your phone number on screen. When calling is available, the platform connects the call.
4. You get updates as the status changes. Check **Alerts** if your account shows them, and check email.

You do not pick the partner from an admin screen. You describe the job; the platform matches a partner.

### 5.4 Track it in My Requests

1. Sign in.
2. Open **Services → My Requests**.
3. You see service bookings, loan applications, and job-consultancy requests together.
4. Open one row to see the status, dates, and any notes.
5. Status labels tell you whether it is waiting, in progress, or finished. Use the filters on the page if you have many requests.

If something looks stuck, use the **Contact** page and mention the request so support can find it.

## Chapter 6. For property agents

You use the same account style as an owner, with two extra menus.

### 6.1 Your day, start to finish

1. Sign in with your phone OTP.
2. Finish onboarding if this is your first login.
3. Open **Overview** to see a snapshot of your work.
4. Post or update listings in **My Listings** using [Chapter 4](#chapter-4-list-a-property). Listings stay **Pending** until they are approved.
5. When a tenant sends an enquiry, open **Sales → Leads**.
6. Read the property and the tenant’s message, then contact them to arrange a visit.
7. Open **Sales → Reports** when you want a summary of enquiries and activity over time.
8. If you book a service for yourself, it appears under **My Requests**.

### 6.2 Leads

Each lead is one enquiry on one of your listings. Open it to see:

- Which property it is for
- The message the person wrote
- When it arrived

Follow up promptly. Update your own **Profile** phone and email so tenants can reach you if the site shows your contact card on the listing.

### 6.3 Reports

**Reports** is a summary for you. It is not a public page. Use it to see how your listings are performing. It does not replace **Leads** for day-to-day follow-up.

### 6.4 If a listing is rejected

Open the listing, read the reason, correct the form, and save. The listing can be reviewed again.

## Chapter 7. For service partners

This is the full path from joining to getting paid.

### 7.1 Create the partner account

1. Open partner registration (not the normal customer register).
2. Enter your name, business name, categories, and mobile number.
3. Verify the OTP.
4. Complete **Complete Your Profile** (email, name, full address).

You are now inside the partner dashboard, but you are not verified yet.

### 7.2 Complete Profile — business

Open **Account → Profile**. Fill **Business profile**:

- **Business name** — the name customers should see.
- **Categories** — search and tick every service you actually offer. Click a badge to remove one.
- **About your business** — what you do, how long you have done it, specialties.
- **Experience** — credentials and past work, in plain sentences.
- **Working hours** — the days and times you work.
- **Service locations** — type an area (for example “South Delhi”) and click **Add area**. Add every city or locality you cover. Remove one with the cross on its badge.
- **Work photos** — up to 10 images of completed jobs (JPG, PNG, or WEBP).
- **Work documents** — up to 5 certificates or PDFs.

Click **Save business profile**.

A **public page** link appears when your profile has an address. Customers can open it **after** you are verified. Until then, treat the link as not ready for sharing.

### 7.3 Complete Profile — documents

On the same Profile area, upload verification files:

- **Aadhaar** (required)
- **PAN** (required)
- **GST** (if you have it)
- **Business proof**
- **Address proof**

Aadhaar and PAN must both be uploaded before verification can succeed. Save the documents.

### 7.4 Wait for verification

Your status shows **Pending**.

- You may be asked to correct something. If the status becomes **Rejected**, read the reason on Profile, replace the files or text, and save again.
- When you are **Approved**, you get an **Alert**. Your public page can then show your business name, about text, locations, hours, photos, and completed jobs.

Do not expect new jobs to be assigned before you are verified.

### 7.5 A new job arrives

1. Open **Jobs & payments → Leads**.
2. A new assignment may show as **pending review**. You can see that a job exists. You should wait until it is approved before you treat it as yours to accept.
3. You also get an **Alert** when you are assigned. The alert alone does not mean you should start the work.
4. The customer’s phone number is **not** listed on the lead.

### 7.6 Accept the job

When the lead is **open**:

1. Open the lead.
2. Read the service, area, preferred date, and any notes.
3. **Accept** if you can do the work. You can also decline if you cannot.

After you accept, the status becomes **accepted**.

### 7.7 Call the customer

When secure calling is turned on for your account:

1. Open the accepted lead.
2. Click **Call customer**.
3. Your phone rings first. Answer it.
4. You are then connected to the customer.
5. The number you both see is the platform line, not each other’s private mobile number.

If **Call customer** is missing, calling is not available for that lead yet. Use the notes on the lead and wait for the button, or contact **Support**. Do not ask the customer to send their number in a public review.

### 7.8 Do the work

On the lead page:

- Add a **milestone** with a short note and photos of progress.
- Mark the lead **in progress** when you have started.
- Mark it **completed** when the work is finished.

Keep notes factual: what you did, and on which date.

### 7.9 Get paid

1. After the job is completed and a job amount is recorded, open **Wallet**.
2. You see earning lines and any commission lines.
3. When the platform pays you out, the wallet balance and history update.
4. You also get an alert when a payout or important status change happens.

You do not type the payout yourself. If a completed job has no amount yet, the amount is added from the platform side and then shows in your wallet. If it is missing for a long time, open **Support** and mention the lead.

### 7.10 Support and alerts

**Alerts** — verification, new leads, approvals, wallet updates, and replies on tickets.

**Support** — create a ticket when you have a payment question or a complaint. Choose a clear subject, describe the lead or the date, and send it. When someone replies, you see the note on the ticket and an alert.

### 7.11 If your account is blocked

If the platform deactivates your account:

- You cannot accept new leads.
- Your public partner page is hidden.

Contact support from the details you were given, or use the **Contact** page, and explain your business name and phone number.

### 7.12 Partner checklist

- Phone OTP account created with the correct business name
- Email and address saved in onboarding
- Categories and service areas saved
- Aadhaar and PAN uploaded
- Status is **Approved** before you share your public page
- You accept a lead only after it is **open**
- You use **Call customer** instead of asking for a private number
- You add photos on the job before you mark it completed
- You check **Wallet** after completion

## Chapter 10. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account to look at homes?**  
No. You can open approved listings from the home page. You need an account to send an enquiry, save a request, or post a listing.

**Why is there no password?**  
Sign-in uses your mobile number and a one-time code by SMS.

**Why is my listing still Pending?**  
It is waiting for review. It is not a public listing until the status changes to Approved. You will get an email when it is approved or rejected.

**Can I edit a listing after it is approved?**  
Yes. Open it from **My Listings**, change the fields, and click **Update Property**. A large change may be reviewed again.

**My listing was rejected. What do I do?**  
Open the listing or the email, read the reason, fix those fields, and save.

**I am an owner. Do I need an agent account?**  
No. Register as a normal user, then use **My Listings → Add Property**.

**Why can’t I see the customer’s phone on a job?**  
The customer’s number stays hidden. After you accept the job, use **Call customer**. Your phone rings, then you are connected. If the button is not there, calling is not available on that job yet.

**When do I get paid for a job?**  
After the job is completed and the amount is recorded, the earning appears in **Wallet**. A payout from the platform also appears in the wallet history. Use **Support** if a completed job never shows an amount.

**How do I become a partner?**  
Use partner registration, then finish Profile (business details, areas, Aadhaar, and PAN) and wait until the status is Approved.

**The form will not go to the next step.**  
The first step needs both a sector and a listing type. Later steps need every field marked required. The page names the missing field.

**I chose Independent House and the floor question disappeared.**  
That is expected. Houses ask how many floors the house has. Apartments ask which floor you are on and how many floors the building has.

**I chose Only lease and rent disappeared.**  
That is expected. Enter the lease amount instead of monthly rent and deposit.

**Sale deed is No and a new question appeared.**  
Answer whether you have a sale agreement.

**Can I put my phone number in the description?**  
No. Write about the property only. Contact happens through the enquiry or your account.

**Who do I contact?**  
Use the **Contact** page on the website. Partners can also open **Support** inside the dashboard. Legal terms are on **Terms and conditions**.
